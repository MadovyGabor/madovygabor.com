01 / System Architecture & Hardware Topology

Addressable fire alarm systems (EPS — Elektronická požiarna signalizácia) represent critical life-safety infrastructure where zero failure tolerance is mandated. This project involved designing the addressable LSN loop topology, calculating physical loop parameters and standby battery autonomy, programming the complex input/output cause-and-effect matrix using Bosch RPS/FSP software, and performing certified §21 commissioning.

02 / Engineering Calculations & Programming Logic

Loop Sizing, Power Autonomy & Cause-and-Effect Logic

Deploying a certified life-safety system requires rigorous mathematical validation before software configuration. Loop resistance, capacitive attenuation, and battery discharge rates must be verified according to national fire safety standards.

1. Electrical Loop Parameters & Resistance

The addressable loop utilizes certified fire-resistant twisted-pair cable ($2 \times 0.8\text{ mm}$ shielded, $S = 0.5\text{ mm}^2$). Electrical specifications:

  • Specific Resistance: $\approx 36.6\,\Omega/\text{km}$ per conductor ($73.2\,\Omega/\text{km}$ loop)
  • Total Loop Length: 720 meters closed ring loop
  • Measured Resistance: $R_{\text{loop}} = 26.4\,\Omega$ ($< 50\,\Omega$ maximum allowed limit)
  • Capacitance: $C_{\text{loop}} = 82\text{ nF}$ ($< 500\text{ nF}$ max LSN limit)

2. Battery Autonomy Sizing (EN 54-4)

The primary mains power supply (230V AC) is backed up by dual 12V 28Ah VRLA batteries wired in series (24V DC nominal). Required formula:

C_req = 1.25 × (I_quiescent × 24h + I_alarm × 0.5h)

• $I_{\text{quiescent}} = 380\text{ mA}$ (Panel CPU + 64x LSN detectors + interface cards)
• $I_{\text{alarm}} = 2.45\text{ A}$ (Full acoustic sirens + optical strobes + relays energized)
• $C_{\text{req}} = 1.25 \times (0.38 \times 24 + 2.45 \times 0.5) = 1.25 \times (9.12 + 1.225) = \mathbf{12.93\text{ Ah}}$
• Installed Capacity: $\mathbf{28\text{ Ah}}$ — Margin of safety: >216%.

3. Cause-and-Effect Programming Matrix (Bosch RPS / FSP)

Using the Bosch RPS configuration suite, inputs and outputs were structured into an automated two-stage verification matrix:

Confirmed Smoke / Thermal (Type B) Two detectors or >60s continuous Full loop sounders & strobes activated HVAC emergency shutoff; Elevator ground recall

verified Certified Electrotechnical Execution (§21 Compliance)

As a certified electrotechnician under §21 (Odborná spôsobilosť v elektrotechnike), all electrical terminations were executed to strict industrial safety standards: halogen-free flame-retardant cable conduits, dedicated 10A B-curve unswitched power supplies, equipotential bonding to the main protective earth (PE) terminal with resistance $R_{\text{PE}} < 0.1\,\Omega$, and separation of low-voltage fire loops from mains power lines to prevent electromagnetic induction.

03 / Commissioning & Stress Testing Protocols

TEST 01

Short-Circuit Isolator Ring Continuity Test

EN 54-13 Integrity
Fault Simulation

Simulated a hard zero-ohm short circuit between positive and negative loop conductors between detector node #24 and manual pull station #25 while the system was under full quiescent polling.

check_circle Automated Isolation Response

Integral LSN isolators in nodes #24 and #25 tripped within 85 milliseconds, isolating the faulted cable span. The control panel immediately switched to bidirectional dual-feed loop driving, maintaining 100% communication with all 64 addressable elements with zero packet drops.

TEST 02

Calibrated Aerosol Smoke & Thermal Response Test

Sensor Verification
Physical Stimulation

Applied certified aerosol smoke (Solo A5) and direct calibrated heat flow (Solo 461) to verify individual sensor response times, optical chamber obscuration thresholds, and address matching against engineering floorplans.

check_circle Calibration & Address Verification

All FAP-425-DOTC sensors responded within 12 to 18 seconds. Point address, zone nomenclature, and room labels matched the CAD floorplan with 100% accuracy. Drift compensation values were recorded and verified at <5% chamber contamination.

TEST 03

Life Safety Relay Latching & HVAC Interlock Verification

Automation Interlock
Multi-System Trigger

Triggered full building alarm to verify simultaneous electrical latching of output relays controlling ventilation dampers, elevator controllers, and access control power supplies.

check_circle Fail-Safe Execution

Air handling units tripped within 0.4 seconds; elevator cars safely transitioned to the primary egress floor with doors held open; electromagnetic access doors dropped voltage immediately, ensuring safe unhindered evacuation paths throughout the facility.
