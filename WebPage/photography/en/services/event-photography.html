FLEXIBILITY & RELIABILITY

Genuine moments captured without awkward posing

Discreet presence and authentic emotions

I primarily photograph events across the Galanta, Dunajská Streda, Šaľa, Nové Zámky, and Trnava regions, including surrounding towns such as Dolné Saliby and Horné Saliby. My goal is to blend seamlessly into the background without interrupting the celebration or pulling guests out of the moment, allowing me to capture honest candid memories. I am always open to spontaneous group portraits and fun creative ideas on site.

Seamless delivery and complete gallery access

Finished photo collections are delivered in full quality through a clean password-protected online Google Drive gallery. I believe in giving you complete freedom of choice, providing a generous and carefully curated selection where everyone finds their favorite shots without compromising professional post-processing standards.
