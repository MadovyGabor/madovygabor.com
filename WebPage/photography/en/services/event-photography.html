FLEXIBILITY & PROCESS

Genuine moments, without unnecessary complications

Discreet presence & authentic atmosphere

I primarily photograph events across the Galanta, Dunajská Streda, Šaľa, and Nové Zámky regions, including surrounding towns (Dolné Saliby, Horné Saliby). My goal is to blend seamlessly into the background: never interrupting the celebration or pulling you out of the moment, allowing me to capture honest, candid memories. Seamless delivery & freedom of choice

Finished photo collections are delivered in full quality via a clean, password-protected online gallery (Google Drive). I believe in giving you freedom of choice, providing a generous, meticulously curated selection where everyone finds their favorite shots – without compromising professional post-processing standards.
