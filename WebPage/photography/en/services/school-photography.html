Class photography should be smooth, punctual, and completely stress-free for both teachers and students. ORGANIZATION & RELIABILITY

Punctual execution and zero administrative burden

Smooth class schedule without teaching disruption

I know how valuable school hours are, so every photoshoot is planned with precision. Photography takes place according to an agreed timetable, with each class requiring only ten to fifteen minutes in the school yard or gym. Teachers do not have to worry about complicated logistics or handling orders on the day. I bring portable professional backdrops and lighting directly to your school for indoor sessions, while outdoor courtyard photos benefit from fresh natural daylight.

Transparent per-student pricing and digital archive

Pricing is completely clear with no unexpected surprise costs. Every participating student receives one physical print in high quality, while class teachers receive a complimentary copy. Schools can also request our Digital School Archive, providing high-resolution digital files through a private cloud gallery for school chronicles, official websites, yearbooks, and promotional materials.

QUESTIONS & DETAILS

Frequently Asked Questions

Key logistical details regarding class scheduling, photo distribution, and institutional archives.

The schedule is designed to minimize classroom disruption. Each class requires only ten to fifteen minutes. We call classes down to the courtyard, gym, or hall in orderly succession so the rest of the school continues their regular curriculum without interruption.

Every participating student receives one physical 13×18 cm group photograph printed on durable silk photo paper. Class headteachers and assistants receive complimentary copies for their personal memories.

When you add the digital school archive, the school administration receives all retouched class photographs in full resolution through a private Google Drive gallery. These files are licensed for the school website, yearbooks, newsletters, and social media channels.

No minimum is required. We photograph individual classes as well as entire elementary schools, kindergartens, and high schools. Tiered per-student pricing automatically adjusts to reward larger student bodies.

We eliminate administrative stress for teachers. Finished printed photographs are neatly packaged class by class and labeled clearly. The package is delivered to the school office within two weeks alongside a clear tally sheet.

Travel is completely free throughout the districts of Galanta, Dunajská Streda, Šaľa, and Nové Zámky. For schools in Trnava, Bratislava, or Nitra, visits are scheduled upon prior arrangement.
